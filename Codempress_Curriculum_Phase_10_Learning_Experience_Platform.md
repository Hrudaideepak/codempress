# Codempress Curriculum v1

# Phase 10 --- Learning Experience Platform (LXP) & Community Ecosystem

> Phase 10 transforms Codempress from an AI mentor into a complete
> engineering ecosystem where learners study, build, collaborate,
> compete, and get hired.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Vision

Learning should not happen alone.

Learners should be able to:

-   Learn
-   Build
-   Collaborate
-   Compete
-   Teach
-   Get Mentored
-   Showcase
-   Get Hired

All inside Codempress.

# Gamification (Meaningful)

Reward:

-   Skill mastery
-   Project completion
-   Helpful reviews
-   Community contributions
-   Consistency
-   Mentoring others

Avoid rewarding low-value activity like endless login streaks.
